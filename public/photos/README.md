# Word photo pack

Photographs downloaded from Pixabay and visually screened for clear subjects on white backgrounds. Source pages and search tags are recorded in review.json and the localWordPhotos manifest. Further therapist review is recommended before clinical use. Missing or rejected targets retain the existing online photo lookup; this pack does not cover the entire curriculum.

These files are bundled with the Android web build and precached by the installed PWA after its initial online installation/update. Local image decoding still takes time; no zero-millisecond guarantee is made.
